## How updates become decisions

1. A counterpart proposes a correction, addition, or different reporting boundary through the existing email thread.
2. TA39 records the proposed change without treating it as agreed.
3. The responsible parties confirm the wording or operational decision.
4. TA39 updates the authoritative Markdown, this decision log, and the change log in the same reviewed change.
